Every corner of Canadian governance
tracked and connected

Think of CivInt like a newsroom that never sleeps — it reads every public document so you don't have to, and shows you what matters.

🏛️

Parliament — HoC & Senate

Every House of Commons and Senate committee meeting, every witness, every question. Detects in-camera sessions across both chambers so you know when debate goes behind closed doors.

📜

Regulations — Canada Gazette

Proposed and final regulations from Canada Gazette Parts I+II. See what rules the government is planning before they become law — from environmental assessments to banking regulations.

💰

Corporate influence

Lobbying reports, government contracts, and executive statements from Canada's biggest banks, telecoms, and media companies. See which companies meet with which departments and which contracts go to single bidders.

🗳️

Elections & Lobbying

Third-party election advertising, political financing, and Ontario provincial lobbying. Track who's spending to influence voters and which organizations are lobbying at the provincial level.

🌐

Influence networks

From the UN to the WEF, from think tanks to NGOs — track organizations that shape Canadian policy. Monitor their government funding, their lobbying, and their connections to decision-makers.

🔍

Oversight & Courts

Audits from the OAG and PBO, Ethics Commissioner investigations, Competition Bureau actions, CRTC decisions, and CanLII court rulings. Full judicial and accountability tracking.

🔗

Connect the dots

The same people show up everywhere — sitting on committees, lobbying departments, winning contracts, appearing in court cases. CivInt links them together so you can see the full picture. Our signal detectors flag revolving doors, lobbying overlaps, and cross-jurisdictional connections automatically.

🍁

Built for Canadians, running in Canada

Every piece of information comes from official public sources — Parliament, the Lobbying Registry, the Canada Gazette, open court data, oversight bodies. Everything is verified and citable. No spin, no opinion, just the facts that are already public, organized so you can actually use them.
