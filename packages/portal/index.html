Three ways to keep tabs
on your government

Think of CivInt like a newsroom that never sleeps — it reads every public document so you don't have to, and shows you what matters.

🏛️

What's happening in Parliament

Every House of Commons committee meeting, every witness, every question. See who spoke, what they said, and which meetings happen behind closed doors (in-camera) — the ones they don't want you to see.

💰

Who's talking to the government

Lobbying reports, government contracts, and CEO statements from Canada's biggest banks, telecoms, and media companies. See which companies are meeting with which departments, and which contracts go to single bidders.

🌐

Who's shaping the rules

From the UN to the WEF, from McKinsey to the Canadian Chamber of Commerce — 32 major organizations that influence Canadian policy. Track their government funding, their lobbying, and their connections to decision-makers.

🔗

Connect the dots

The same people show up everywhere — sitting on committees, lobbying departments, winning contracts. CivInt links them together so you can see the full picture. Our 7 signal detectors flag things like revolving doors and lobbying overlaps automatically.

🍁

Built for Canadians, running in Canada

Every piece of information comes from official public sources — Parliament, the Lobbying Registry, open government data. Everything is verified and citable. No spin, no opinion, just the facts that are already public, organized so you can actually use them.

Use it with ChatGPT, Claude,
Cursor — or just your browser

CivInt connects to the AI tools you already use. Ask questions about Canadian government data in plain English and get answers with sources you can verify.

Claude / ChatGPT

Add CivInt as a tool in your AI assistant. Ask "what did the ethics committee discuss yesterday?" and get a sourced answer.

}}
